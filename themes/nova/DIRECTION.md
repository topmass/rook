# Nova

A nova is a star that flares to many times its brightness, then settles. That is this theme: Rook as a small star
that answers with bursts of plasma. It is the loud, crafted sibling of Devs.

## What light is made of

Light here is star plasma, drawn like hand-animated effects in anime.

- **Plasma** fills every shape. It has a white-hot core, an electric violet body and a deep indigo edge.
- **Cel bands, not gradients.** Brightness steps in three or four hard bands. The steps give the crafted look.
- **Ink.** Every solid shape has a thin deep-indigo outline and a dark body. Shapes read as objects, not as haze.
- **Cyan rim.** Edges that face away from the core catch a thin cyan light. It separates form from glow.
- **Sparks are streaks.** A spark is a short tapered line along its motion, never a soft dot.
- **One warm accent.** Amber marks time (the timer). Hot rose marks trouble (the error). Nothing else is warm.

## How it moves

Nova moves in snaps. A shape anticipates, bursts, then settles, like a hit in a fight scene. The icons play on twos,
at 12 frames a second, as hand-drawn effects do. Spins and flows are steady; flares are sudden. Every loop is exact,
so a loop never jumps.

Devs is the opposite. Its gold dust drifts slowly and softly, with no outlines and no bands. Devs whispers; Nova
flares. Both keep the same Rook silhouette and the same meaning for each state.

## Change between icons

A change never pops. The old icon shrinks a little and fades as the new one grows from 85% to full size. A small
star flash, one quarter of a second, covers the seam. Thinking always goes rook, other icon, rook, other icon.

## Dark and light desktops

On a dark desktop the glow carries the icon. On a light desktop the ink body and outline carry it, like a sticker.
The glow then reads as a soft violet halo. Every icon must pass both checks before it counts.
